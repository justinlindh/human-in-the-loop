# Content ladder proposal (Phase 6, S19)

Status: proposal for the lead and the user to review. Nothing marked NEW is built yet.

## How to read this

- The run is 1040 weeks (2019 to 2039). Balance is in weeks, so the ladder is in weeks too.
- At 8 s per week and 1x, one week is about 0.13 real minutes before pauses. With decision and menu pauses a game year runs about 12 to 15 minutes, so a rough conversion is **real minutes ≈ week × 0.25**. The minute column uses that and is only a guide.
- Target: something new every 10 to 20 real minutes (about every 40 to 80 weeks) for the whole run, and never more than 2 unlock cards in a real minute outside era arrivals.
- **Trigger** is what makes the item arrive. "World" means the fixed timeline (with per-run jitter where noted). "Progress" means the player's company has to earn it. Most entries are progress-gated with a world floor, so a slow company still sees the ladder and a fast one cannot skip ahead of the story.
- **Status:** EXISTS (already in the sim), CHANGE (exists, retimed or reworked), NEW (to build).
- **Lanes:** S sim, U ui, A art. The sim lane owns data and rules; ui presents cards, panels and toasts; art builds anything visible in the diorama.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Campus as a fourth stage** (week ~620): it gives the 2030s a physical goal, but it is the most art work on the list (a new stage shell, a bigger grid, a second building). Alternative: an HQ expansion (knock through a wall into the neighbouring floor) that reuses the HQ shell. Recommendation: the HQ expansion, which is cheaper and still gives a goal.
2. **Lockdown**: 10 to 20 weeks is 3 to 5 real minutes with the office empty. The video-call grid keeps the diorama alive; the alternative is a shorter 6-week version. Recommendation: 10 weeks, with the work policy decision as the real payload.
3. **Meaning hidden in Classic** (issue #7): hiding it changes the Staff panel and several tooltips, and it retires the Classic meaning grind as a visible stat. Recommendation: yes. The Classic hour is simpler for it, and the ChatGBT card gets a real story turn.
4. **The rival company**: new state (a small rival entity with a name, a category, a strength curve). It is the ladder's main recurring character across 20 years. Recommendation: yes.
