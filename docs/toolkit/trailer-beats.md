Replays every trailer and landing-page beat's capture setup against the sim alone (no browser, no Vite, no render lock) and prints the moment each one lands on, so a sim change that moves a beat shows up in the sim PR instead of in video's rebuild. The beats are every item in `scripts/trailer/manifest.js`, every trailer pin's source (`pin-*`, from `scripts/trailer/pin-manifest.js`) and every `site-*` item in `scripts/feature-media/manifest.js` that has a sim setup; an item that opens at an indexed `moment` is skipped, since the event index finds it again after a sim change. A setup runs as written, with DOM and renderer calls as no-ops and `LOAD_PIN` reading its `.snap` through the game's own save. The moment is where the setup leaves the game (week, era, office stage, headcount, pending decision, outage) plus what the next week's tick raises (decision, outage, incident, robot, era and launch events, and during an outage how many replies a meme posted then would draw, which the Yak beat needs two of), since `PRE_UNTIL` and a pin both stop the week before the beat's subject. A `pin-*` line says `source drifted from the pin` when its source replay no longer lands where the stored snapshot does. That is information, not a break: the pinned beats load the snapshot, so their own lines (the next tick's decision, outage and meme replies) are what must hold, and video decides whether to re-pin with `scripts/trailer/pin.mjs`. A setup that throws prints `ERROR` with its message (a `RUNAWAY` that finds no cloud bill, say). All beats take about 20 s.

On a sim PR, compare with main: `scripts/tools/ab.sh -- node tests/sim/trailer-beats/replay.mjs` prints only the beats whose moment moved. For a base older than this script, pass a copy of it by absolute path with `--root .`, which replays the base checkout's sim and beats with this script. Paste the diff into the PR and list video under Affects for every moved, stale or broken beat.
