For held props, scene rules accept `heldHeadDepth`, `heldTorsoDepth` and `heldGap`, in metres. Penetration uses the sweep's mesh tests with no tolerance; gap measures the wrist pivot to the prop surface. The measures cover all props carried by the selected person, taking the worst depth or gap. No prop gives null, so an absent grip cannot pass. Sample every frame with `--every 1`; start sampling when pickup has happened, and use `--expect 'heldHeadDepth<=0.000001' --expect 'heldTorsoDepth<=0.000001' --expect 'heldGap<=0.04'`. For the named hammer meshes, `heldPalmGap` measures the primary palm to the handle centreline, `heldSupportGap` the supporting palm to the shaft, `heldHeadDistance` the palm to the head centre, and `heldHeadJoint` the head to the end of the handle, all in metres. Use palm gaps <= 0.02, head distance 0.35 to 0.6, and joint gap <= 0.08. `heldHandleVisible` and `heldHeadVisible` ray-test the parts separately against the scene, including the holder, and reject offscreen samples. `heldShaftProjection` is the shaft length in the camera plane divided by its world length (>= 0.6 avoids an end-on handle). `heldHeadCross` is the head width perpendicular to the projected shaft divided by shaft length (>= 0.25 preserves the cross silhouette). `heldScreenDistance` measures the head-to-palm distance in the camera plane, in world metres, independent of zoom. Missing or hidden parts return null. These are geometric checks, not a substitute for inspecting the hold and its transitions. Rule output includes minimum and maximum values. Mock scenes accept `--rig off` for procedural animation.

Named targets use the bare head geometry in its local frame, transformed with the animated head. `hand0EyeLeft` and `hand0EyeRight` measure hand-centre distance to the head surface under each modelled eye centre (left is negative head-local x). `hand0Eye` selects the nearer eye. `hand0Brow` selects the nearer point at the upper edge of either eye; `hand0Forehead` is halfway from brow height to the head top. `hand0Mouth` uses the neutral mouth centre; `hand0Chin` is halfway from mouth height to the head bottom. All have `hand1` equivalents. These are point distances in metres, not distances to a broad region. Hats, hair and facial expressions do not move the targets. The original Face, Head and HeadTop surface measures remain available. Use `--expect 'hand0Eye<=0.04@0.8' --expect 'hand0Brow<=0.04@0.8'` for an upper-face gesture; a chin touch cannot pass those bounds. Geometric proximity does not prove a readable pose: inspect a final capture.
