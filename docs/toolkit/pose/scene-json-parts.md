- Each geometric part has `status`, `rect`, `clippedRect` and `clipped`. Rectangles have `left/right/top/bottom`. Triangles are clipped to camera near/far planes before perspective division for `rect`; x/y can extend outside the canvas. `clippedRect` bounds those triangles after all six frustum planes, including intersections whose original vertices are all outside. `clipped` means some source geometry crosses or lies beyond a clip plane. `projected` means geometry intersects the clip volume, not that its pixels are visible. `offscreen` has null `clippedRect`; `rect` is also null when no geometry survives near/far clipping. Degenerate edge-on geometry may have zero width/height; missing geometry never gets a fabricated zero rectangle.
- `hidden` has both rectangles null: a mesh/sprite or ancestor is invisible, its camera layer is excluded, its material is invisible, or its transparent material has zero opacity. Hidden hands remain explicitly identified. A selected subject with no eligible visible head row remains absent, so the existing missing-frame failure still applies. Missing/duplicate arm tags, absent template geometry, unsupported baked topology, nonfinite coordinates or invalid canvas dimensions raise errors. Hand extraction validates arm-then-hand triangle counts and source shape against the chibi template before trusting the range; it does not guess a hand box from an arm's bounds.
- `overlays` includes all eligible scene DOM speech bubbles (`bubble`), stat labels (`stat`), other floating labels (`label`), and character emote sprites. It is repeated per subject so another person's overlay is available when checking a face. Each entry has a stable `id`, `subjectId`, `kind`, `text`, the rectangle/status fields and `overlapsFace`. That flag means positive rectangle overlap with this row's exact clipped face bounds. It does not imply depth occlusion or ownership. Emotes identify their subject and sprite; DOM labels have null `subjectId` because the renderer does not expose their follow target. Do not infer a speaker from proximity or text.
- DOM label IDs track pooled element identity for this measuring session, independent of text/order, and may be reused for a later utterance when the renderer recycles an element. They are not cross-run utterance IDs. DOM rectangles reuse `screen()` measurements of the inner element after the last render, including its CSS scale/translation; they omit pseudo-element tails, shadows and leader lines. The existing eligibility policy omits disconnected/display-none labels, opacity below 0.6, and dimensions below one CSS pixel. An empty overlay list means none met that policy. Labels are clipped to the canvas rectangle; emote quads use camera-facing sprite geometry including center, scale, material rotation and perspective size attenuation. Hidden emotes have null bounds, not a fabricated box. Text retains the existing 40-character diagnostic limit.

These bounds establish projected geometric extent and rectangular overlap only. They include empty corners and transparent texture regions, do not evaluate shader displacement, backface culling, depth occlusion, pixel coverage, font legibility or semantic readability, and cannot prove a hand covers an eye. Keep facial landmark visibility/contact rules and final capture inspection for those questions. The scene harness draws nothing and still takes the render lock; no subsecond latency is claimed.
