## Screen-space cover: does A hide B from the camera

Head-relative numbers (`hand0Eye`, "the palm is 9 cm in front of the eye") do not say what the camera sees: with the head turned away, a palm in front of the eye can sit beside it on screen. The cover measures answer with rays from the camera. A measure is `cover<A><B>`, usable as a scene rule or listed with `--cover` (comma separated, or repeated) to print it without judging it:

- **A**: `Hand` (either hand's triangles, not the arm or sleeve), `HandL`, `HandR` (the rig's left and right, never swapped by the camera), or `Bubble` (any scene DOM speech bubble, label or emote rectangle, by screen position).
- **B**: `EyeNear` (the eye whose landmark is nearer the camera along its view, so the camera-side eye is picked from the head's own facing), `EyeFar`, `EyeL`, `EyeR`, or `Face` (the six landmark groups `faceVisible` uses).

Each eye is sampled where the camera sees it: the character's drawn eye (its facial ink, split at the head's middle into the two eyes, so a closed or narrowed eye is as thin as it is drawn) with its landmark on the ink's own surface, and 19 points on an ellipse of the eye's own width and height around it, in the face's plane so a turned head foreshortens them, spread evenly by area (the centre, a ring at half size, a ring at 85%). The model's eye geometry stands in when the ink has no separate eye. `Face` uses the six landmark groups `faceVisible` uses, with half-eye patches around each. A sample is covered when the ray from the camera to it meets A's triangles nearer the camera than the head's own surface, so a palm inside the head, behind its surface, covers nothing. The value is the covered share, 0 to 1, with off-screen samples counted as uncovered. When the head faces the camera the two eyes are level (their depth differs by under a quarter of their spacing) and neither is the camera side, so `EyeNear` uses whichever eye the hand covers more, and `of` says so. Rules use it like any scene measure and hold it to a share of the requested frames: `--expect 's2:coverHandEyeNear>=0.5@0.7'` says the palm covers at least half of the camera-side eye in 70% of the gesture. Each printed line and each `--json` row carries `covers.<name>` with `fraction`, `front` (`a`: A in front of at least half of B; `partial`; `b`: nothing of A in front), `of` (which eye or `Face`), `rings` (the share covered at the centre, the middle ring and the outer ring, which shows a palm that reaches the middle but misses an edge), `eyeHalfSize` (the sampled ellipse, head-local metres) and how many samples were on screen. A hand behind the head, hidden behind another person's mesh in a way this ray misses, or a bubble (always drawn over the scene) are limits to read from the numbers, not from a rule alone: confirm with a capture.

`node blender/checks/pose-cover-controls.mjs` runs the controls (a facepalm passes, the same person typing fails with B clear, left and right are told apart, the far eye is a separate target, an unknown measure is refused), about half a minute.
