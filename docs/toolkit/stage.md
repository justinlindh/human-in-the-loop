`--touched` runs the specs a change adds or edits against a base (default `origin/main`), plus every spec of a scenario whose setup changed, so a new spec is not left unrun by an `--only` that names the old ones; it prints each spec it picked and why, and says so and exits 0 when none is touched. Specs are compared as written in `stage.mjs` (an edited comment above a spec counts). `--only` (also given with `--touched`, which adds to it) exits 2 on a name that matches no spec instead of running nothing. Run `stage.mjs --touched` before asking for review on a PR that adds or changes a moment's spec.

The hammer spec measures every held frame of carry, hold and swing separately in both camera views. Head and torso penetration must be at most one micrometre; both palms must stay within 2 cm of the shaft. Head distance, handle connection, screen proximity, separate shaft/head visibility and the projected cross silhouette also have rules. Both parts must be visible on every hold and swing frame, and at least 85% of carry frames to allow brief scenery occlusion while walking. The projected silhouette must pass at least 95% of each beat. The swing is triggered through the decision event.

The staging probe (#350): does each character moment read on screen? Plays every moment from the default camera and a turned view, samples `R.probe(id)` every frame, splits the samples by beat and holds each beat to its readability spec. Prints a per-beat table (check, view, beat, metric, value, want) and writes `shots/stage/report.json`. A moment this build doesn't play is skipped. It fails any role a moment stages (a staff role or a visitor) that no spec covers, so an actor nobody wrote a rule for cannot pass unchecked. A rule marked `known: <issue>` fails as KNOWN without failing the run until that issue is fixed: closed by a merged PR or commit that changed game code (`src/` or `public/`) (checked with `gh` once per run, and part of the check cache's key; unreachable counts as open). Then it fails the run again. An issue closed any other way (by hand, or by a PR that only mentions it) keeps its rules excused, and the run says to reopen it. It says so when a known rule passes again. Local CI runs it on a GPU slot for PRs that touch `src/render/`, `public/models/` or the staging check and its helpers. See "Writing a readability spec" below.

When a readability spec fails, the check prints spot-search summaries from `R.debug.spots`: rejected requirements with counts, the chosen point, and whether it was a fallback. Use the dump's `spotSearches` for the individual candidates. This also prints for failures excused by a known issue.

The `fumes` specs measure its fanning beat from both views. `growth` checks the honoree and coworkers during a career celebration; `company_party` checks the company cheer.

`--only=pet,petcat` measures upright turning, the pet approach, and head-scratch contact and visibility from both views. The fixture uses incoming yaw 2.104 and enough separation to exercise each phase. Scenario `setup` expressions can await fixture imports before sampling. `--only=robot` holds the office robot's fixer to facing it in the wind-up, the right hand landing within 0.06 m of its head, and both of them in view, from both views.

In the report, `facingCamera` is the share of a beat's frames whose face points within the spec's angle of the camera (the probe's `faceCam`). It says nothing about what hides the face: that is `pose.mjs --scene`'s `faceVisible`, the share of facial landmarks the camera sees.
