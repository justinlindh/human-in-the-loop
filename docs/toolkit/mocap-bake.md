The bake stage of the motion pipeline (#1404): one person of a tracked shot (`mocap-track` output) in, one named clip for the chibi rig out. Without `--person` every person is baked, one file each, into the `--out` directory; `--out` ending in `.json` takes one person. `--from` and `--to` cut a range of the shot's frames (source rate). It reads the rig's pivot heights from `public/models/chibi_rig.glb` and takes the bone list from the same names as `src/render/rig.js`; nothing else from the game.

Clip, schema `hitl-mocap-clip` version 1, JSON:
- `name`, `fps` (30, resampled from the shot with slerp), `frames`, `bones` (`body, hips, legL, legR, torso, head, armL, armR`), `scale` (the chibi's leg length over the source person's: multiply source metres by it), `source: { shot, trackId, start, end }` (frames of the video; no paths).
- `tracks[bone].quat`: per frame `[x, y, z, w]`, the change from rest in the parent's frame, as `rig.js` convert() builds it. `tracks.body.pos`: metres at chibi scale, y up, relative to frame 0.
- `filter`: the smoothing applied, `{ type: 'one-euro', minCutoff, beta, dCutoff }`, or null for a raw bake. Every rotation, the root path (`body.pos`), `look` and `bend` go through a one-euro filter (`filter.mjs`) at the clip's 30 fps, run forward and backward and averaged so it adds no lag, with quaternion sign continuity and renormalising. Defaults `minCutoff` 1.5 Hz (lower is smoother when slow) and `beta` 0.3 (higher keeps fast motion crisper); `--smooth off` bakes raw, `--smooth <minCutoff>` and `--beta <k>` change them. On the printer proof shot the defaults cut the mean angular acceleration of the played rig by about 60% for a mean 1.2 degrees of difference from the raw rotations.
- Space: origin on the floor under the hips at frame 0 (floor is the 2nd percentile of the toe height), +y up, +z the way the hips face at frame 0, +x to the left of a body facing +z. The rig's `L` limb is at -x, so it is the person's anatomical right limb. The body bone carries the heading (yaw of the hips); hips, torso and head carry the rest.
- Limbs are single segments: a leg is the hip-to-ankle direction, an arm shoulder-to-wrist (the swing from hanging straight down, no twist). `bend` per limb (`legL, legR, armL, armR`, 0..1) keeps the knee or elbow flexion (150 degrees reads as 1).
- `origin: { pos: [x, 0, z], yaw, scale }` (when the shot has its camera data): the clip's frame-0 origin and its +z heading in one space shared by every clip of the shot: y up, z the first camera's forward flattened onto the floor, x = y cross z, floor at y = 0. Each person's world is gravity-aligned but separate, so the up direction comes through the camera (root orientation in the camera against in its own world); positions are `root_pos_scene` at `origin.scale`, one chibi scale for the whole shot, so distances between people hold. A person's clip body is at its own `scale`.
- `look`: per frame a point 1.5 chibi metres ahead of the head, along the head's facing, in clip space.
- `conf`: per bone and frame, 0..1, the lowest 2D trust over the joints that define the bone, 0 on frames the detector did not see the person.
- `contacts`: `[{ limb: footL|footR|handL|handR, from, to, point }]`, `[from, to)` in clip frames, `point` in clip space (the ankle or wrist at chibi scale, averaged over the span). `point` is the median over the contact. A foot is planted when its toe is within 5 cm of the lowest toe of either foot over the surrounding second (so a drifting floor does not matter) and the ankle moves under 0.6 m/s for 3 frames or more; a contact ends where the limb strays more than 3 cm (source metres) from its running mean, so a step or a slide makes two plants. A hand holds when it moves under 0.2 m/s for 0.3 s with the arm at least 40 degrees off hanging. Both are heuristics from joint motion alone (no scene), so a hand at rest in the air counts as a hold.

Not covered: twist of a limb, fingers, face, and the scene the contacts touch. Tests use a synthetic skeleton, never the reference footage.
