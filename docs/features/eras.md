# Eras and time of day

- **Era art preview**: `?eras&eraArt=<era>` enables the scripted era props. Mock scenes accept `preinternet`, `dotcom`, `dotcom-bust`, `web2`, `classic`, `chatgbt`, `agents`, `consolidation` and `plateau`; add `mock=garage`, `mock=floor` or `mock=hq`. `eraArt=calendar` follows the calendar, and founded era careers always follow their saved calendar and era. The saved dot-com boom/bust phase selects the lease ad when that chapter is present. Ordinary play, `?eras` alone and an invalid art selector load no preview models. Simulation, navigation and era timing are unchanged.
- **Early office props**: pre-internet and dot-com previews use the beige CRT and low divider (`era_crt_desk`, `era_cubicle`). A wall shelf displays original boxed software, diskettes and optical media (`era_retail_boxes`, `era_floppy_stack`, `era_cd_spindle`); it stays through Web 2.0. The media share one small-prop triangle budget. Dot-com adds the static boom/bust graph (`era_dotcom_board`) and wall-mounted Y2K clock and compliance seal (`era_y2k_clock`, `era_y2k_sticker`). The clock face reads 23:59; these are art-preview props, with the rollover staging supplied by the Y2K moment. Web 2.0 adds the framed "Best viewed in Internet Exploder 6" badge (`era_web2_badge`). Wall pieces use the office's available wall slots, clear of tall furniture. The divider is desk dressing, with no noise or focus effect.
- **Roadside calendar**: one billboard occupies the exterior's lawn/plaza position, clear of traffic. Pre-internet has an original Pocket Page pager ad, with a payphone and Rewind Room rental-shop fascia on the neighbouring building (`era_pager_billboard`, `era_payphone`, `era_video_sign`). Dot-com uses the Parcel Paws sock-cat courier (`era_sock_billboard`). Both painted boards have floodlamps on arms, emissive heads and a warm night wash. The bust replaces the board with a backlit "FOR LEASE" box (`era_lease_billboard`); Web 2.0 gets a glossy "beta" box (`era_beta_billboard`). Modern eras use the thin `era_led_billboard`, with Lunch Loop, Draft Buddy, Errand Cloud, One Suite and Made Here ads. The LED face is a static texture updated only when the era changes. All lighting is emissive, with no additional lights; Low retains the era props and cheap exterior shading. Tall scenery follows the exterior's view hiding when the camera turns.
- **Later-era founding**: with `?eras`, ChatGBT and Agents can be selected when founding. The UI reads the presence of this query parameter once at startup (`?eras=1` also works). Without it, new games use Classic and era-start labels stay hidden. The calendar starts at the selected era's seeded arrival, future arrivals keep their relative spacing, and company age begins at zero. The new company does not replay past lockdown or era decisions. Its twentieth anniversary is twenty years after founding. Saves retain their starting era even without the switch; sim tests and the balance harness select eras directly. `id: chatgbt` `id: agents`

- **Era colour**: each era adds a wainscot band and emblem in its colour, a faint wash on hard floors, a tint on the skyline and its own light tone.
- **Era carpets**: the Office Floor and HQ annex use warm neutrals at low contrast, so team mats and people read first: an oatmeal tile grid for Classic, sage-grey pinstripes for ChatGBT, a mauve-beige dot grid for Agents, stone paired stripes for Consolidation and small clay diamonds for Plateau. Every motif runs along the floor's axes, never up the screen. Low quality keeps the patterns and colours with smaller textures and no fibre weave. The garage concrete, HQ wood and tile, and terrace decking keep their material identity. See `?mock=floor` in each era, or the HQ with expansion 3.
- **Day and night**: a real-time day cycle; the sky gradient shifts, windows show a skyline blended by daylight, lit windows appear across the city at night, and interior lamps come on. `?mock=night` `?time=night`
- **Surroundings**: drifting clouds, trees, cars and street lamps round the diorama board, placed so a turned view never hides the office.
- **Lockdown light**: the office dims, plants wilt over the weeks and recover after, and one stayer wanders and naps on the couch. `capture 5-3-lockdown`
