# Eras and time of day

- **Dot-com art trial**: `?eras&eraArt=dotcom` previews a beige CRT workstation, a low modular divider and an original Parcel Paws sock-cat pet-delivery billboard on the garage's front lawn. The billboard has 1990s floodlamps on arms: at night their heads glow and a warm wash lights its face. Both effects are emissive only, with no added light. Add `mock=garage` or `mock=floor` for the review scenes. This explicit art preview swaps desk dressing at every office stage and quality, independently of the simulation era; ordinary play and `?eras` alone keep their usual models. The divider is desk dressing, not a purchasable partition or a noise/focus effect. Model names: `era_crt_desk`, `era_cubicle`, `era_sock_billboard`.
- **Later-era founding**: with `?eras`, ChatGBT and Agents can be selected when founding. The UI reads the presence of this query parameter once at startup (`?eras=1` also works). Without it, new games use Classic and era-start labels stay hidden. The calendar starts at the selected era's seeded arrival, future arrivals keep their relative spacing, and company age begins at zero. The new company does not replay past lockdown or era decisions. Its twentieth anniversary is twenty years after founding. Saves retain their starting era even without the switch; sim tests and the balance harness select eras directly. `id: chatgbt` `id: agents`

- **Era colour**: each era adds a wainscot band and emblem in its colour, a faint wash on hard floors, a tint on the skyline and its own light tone.
- **Era carpets**: the Office Floor and HQ annex use warm neutrals at low contrast, so team mats and people read first: an oatmeal tile grid for Classic, sage-grey pinstripes for ChatGBT, a mauve-beige dot grid for Agents, stone paired stripes for Consolidation and small clay diamonds for Plateau. Every motif runs along the floor's axes, never up the screen. Low quality keeps the patterns and colours with smaller textures and no fibre weave. The garage concrete, HQ wood and tile, and terrace decking keep their material identity. See `?mock=floor` in each era, or the HQ with expansion 3.
- **Day and night**: a real-time day cycle; the sky gradient shifts, windows show a skyline blended by daylight, lit windows appear across the city at night, and interior lamps come on. `?mock=night` `?time=night`
- **Surroundings**: drifting clouds, trees, cars and street lamps round the diorama board, placed so a turned view never hides the office.
