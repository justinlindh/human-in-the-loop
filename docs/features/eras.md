# Eras and time of day

- **Pre-internet founding**: select Pre-internet with `?eras`. Two founders start in a three-desk garage with $240K added to the selected funding, Ops and retail open, no skipped goals and score x1.00. A compressed 1990 to 1997 chapter lasts 156 company weeks, followed by 208 weeks each of dot-com and Web 2.0, then twenty modern years. Dot-com beats count from entry into that chapter. Existing historical saves retain their saved routes and score factors. `id: preinternet`
- **Physical releases**: choose Boxed Software in Build, then use Reports > Inventory, marked with a box icon. Order 100, 500 or 1,000 copies at $8 each before discounts. Delivery takes two playable weeks. Product appeal and health drive demand; sales consume stock at $500 a copy, less the retailer's 30% share. Reviews below 6 produce one return per twenty eligible copies, carrying the remaining eligible units between weeks. Valuation includes the last 52 weeks of box receipts after retailer fees and refunds, using the same annual revenue multiple as service sales. Installed copies remain separate from subscribers and service MRR through every chapter. Start a separate On-prem or web product for recurring service contracts. The six Reports tabs wrap on phones; Build keeps the inventory link above each work tab. `id: boxed`
- **Mastering, shelf clearance and CDs**: each pre-internet boxed release offers master verification for $2K and five reliability/maximum-health points, or a deadline bonus of $3K with three debt. Four weeks after its first delivery, the retailer withdraws remaining stock and charges 20% of its manufacturing cost, capped at $8K, once per product. Chapter week 78 offers CD mastering for $4K; the next batch holds 25% more paid copies. A first delivered physical batch earns $5K and two brand. `id: pre_master_disk` `id: pre_retail_returns` `id: pre_cd_rom` `id: pre_first_batch`
- **Patches by post**: maintenance slows boxed-product wear; restoring health requires a mailed patch at $2 per installed copy, capped at $10K. Reports shows the price, current and restored health, installed release version, stock, deliveries, sales, customer returns, net box receipts, retailer fees, refunds, manufacturing, buyback and patch costs. The patch posts an original DeskNet line. `id: pre_patch_by_post`
- **Market clock**: in every `?eras` career, the market's difficulty follows the calendar from 2019: rival clones, review expectations, project size, market size and the talent pool. Through the early chapters it stays at the 2019 level, so a company that reaches Classic meets the market a fresh Classic founding meets. A ChatGBT or Agents founding meets the market of its calendar year. Company costs (incident bills, tenure, cooldowns, salaries) follow company age. In a Classic career the two clocks are the same. To see it, found a dot-com company and compare new project sizes in 1999 and 2020 with a Classic founding's first year.
