# The millennium watch

- **Paying compliance work:** with `?eras`, found a dot-com company and open Build > Projects during calendar 1999. Each Y2K compliance contract takes 40 work points and pays $18K on completion, with no upfront cost. Assign a team as for other projects. One contract can run at a time, up to three completed contracts per company. Unfinished contracts can finish after the year turns; cancellation pays nothing. Mostly, the work is renaming date fields. `id: y2k_compliance`
- **The volunteer rota:** during the final quarter of calendar 1999, a decision asks the founder to take the pager, the team to share the watch, or Clive to consult at triple his $3K rate. The first two choices cost nothing and do not reduce output. Clive charges $9K once and provides no extra protection. An unaffordable booking is disabled. `id: dotcom_y2k_oncall`
- **The countdown:** in the last company tick whose calendar date is still 1999, before the next maps to 2000, the team gathers around its server rack or a desk computer. A spotlight holds the clock for a five-second gathering, five-second countdown, three seconds of nothing happening, and five seconds of a printer producing an invoice dated 01/01/1900. The caption carries the countdown and invoice date. Skip and fastest speed cut the presentation; Low keeps the printer and captions without gathering walkers. The scene has no cash, customer, morale or outage effect. `id: y2k`
- **AwayIM afterwards:** the following tick posts one thread. Each staff member credits their own preparation, and Clive’s away reply says he has already moved on to e-business transformation. The saved rollover marker prevents duplicate threads. Reloading during the presentation replays that presentation, then continues to the same single aftermath. `id: dotcom`

Stand-ins: the existing `server_rack`, or a desk computer when the company has no rack; the ordinary kitchen `printer` prop and its printer model; a simple invoice sheet; the existing spotlight caption as the countdown display. No special sound plays at rollover. The clock (`era_y2k_clock`), compliance sticker (`era_y2k_sticker`), champagne, party hats and `y2k_rollover` audio cue are art/audio follow-ups on issue #1242. The caption supplies the cheap-champagne joke while those props are absent.

How to see it: with `?eras`, choose The Dot-com Boom, then reach the end of calendar 1999. The default chapter reaches this at the tick from company week 103 to 104. A changed saved chapter duration changes that company week: the trigger follows the calendar. `node blender/checks/stage.mjs --only=y2k` checks the gathering from both views.
