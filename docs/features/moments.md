Staged decisions have short spoken pools in `src/data/moment-talk.js`: the subject, the staged reader and nearby colleagues discuss the prop while its card or Yak prompt is open, then respond to the chosen outcome. - **Moment camera**: during every registered spotlight, the camera glides in and back out, and lets go as soon as the player steers.
- **Spotlight**: `src/render/spotlight-kinds.js` is the shared list (`spotlight: true`): printer smash, user test, consultants, hammer swing, waffle party, music night, and the decision scenes: letter reading, fanning fumes and the pet carrier, each with its own expected length. Each holds the clock and queued notifications while the office animates, follows its action with the optional moment camera, and shows a caption with a tap-sized Skip. Steering releases the camera. At 2x scenes play at real-time speed; at 4x they skip and leave a caption toast. Opening a player menu doesn't cut the scene: it plays on and stops holding the clock. Lingering props spotlight only their first staged animation, and a skipped visitor stays skipped until its prop goes away. Pizza gatherings, screen recoils, company celebrations, career path, Legend and top-level celebrations, standups, coffee and pair games play on without holding the clock or moving the camera. Low keeps its lightweight prop emotes; growth celebrations omit the floor glow and confetti. See letters and fumes through `hearing_summons` and `coffee_machine_broke`; choose a career path, reach Legend or level 20 for the growth scenes. `id: growth` `id: company_party`
