# The office (stages, items, perks)

- ★ **The office move**: the next office drops from above onto the old one, presses it flat, squashes on landing and puffs dust; a stinger plays. Whatever panel or dialog was open closes as the move starts, so it plays in view. When a move becomes possible and stays possible for two game weeks, a Needs you row names it with its price and the Office button shows New; both go once you open Office or tap Later, once per new office. `capture 2-2-office-move`
- **Garage**: a suburban lot with lawn, driveway, picket fences, houses behind, a roll-up garage door and a water heater in the corner. A new company starts with a desk for each founder, so the first hire needs one more. `?mock=garage` `id: place_desks`
- **Office Floor**: a storey of a tower above a plaza among neighbouring buildings, with structural columns that fade when someone stands behind them. `?mock=floor`
- **HQ Building**: a campus plaza with planters, trees, lamps, a road and a skyline. `?mock=hq`
- **HQ expansions**: Knock-through (old wall lines become metal thresholds), Roof Terrace (plank decking, glass balustrade, string lights; outdoor-friendly items only) and The Annex (a carpeted extra wing); each swaps the shell in place with a dust puff. Spoken: "They have measured that wall more than our product." `id: floor_next_door`
- **Build mode**: a tile grid and a tinted ghost; R rotates, adjacency previews glow under what an item would boost, and "Place for me" works without aiming. On touch a tap aims the ghost, a drag that starts on it carries it (any other drag pans), Rotate turns it in place, and a tap on it or the Place button puts it down. `capture 2-1-build-mode`
- **New toys**: something to use (a table game, a couch, the coffee corner, an arcade) that's just been placed draws the nearest free people straight away: a pair table's first game starts within a second, even during a standup. While it waits for takers, nobody else wanders off to another perk.
- **Desk sets**: one person each; a team mat under each set is tinted by the sitter's role, and neighbouring desks butt into a bench. `id: desk`
- **Meeting table**: staged standups happen at it. Attendees walk over and sit in its chairs, speakers first and in the chairs that face the camera. Anyone without a chair stands at the table's ends and far side, clear of the view. Empty chairs tuck in, and when the standup ends everyone gets up from behind their chair and goes back. On Low everyone stands round it with the chairs tucked. With Daily Standups on, everyone in the weekly standup at the table picks up a little knowledge of the systems. `id: meeting_table`
- **Whiteboard**: boosts inventiveness nearby; hard-problem people stand at it thinking. `id: whiteboard`
- **Coffee corner**: a kettle, a drip machine and a "World's Okayest Dev" mug in the description; people stop by to sip. `id: coffee_corner` `id: coffee`
- **Potted plant, bookshelf, couch**: small recovery and learning boosts; people browse the shelf and sit on the couch. `id: plant` `id: bookshelf` `id: couch`
- ★ **Foosball**: two people play head to head. An orange ball runs between the rods, the rods slide after it and whip round when a man kicks it back, and now and then it drops into a goal and a new one pops out at the centre. At the end the winner celebrates with a sparkle and the loser groans. `id: foosball`
- ★ **Ping pong**: a real volley, each player holding the paddle they picked up off the table (the table's paddles come back after), the ball arcing paddle to paddle and bouncing once each side, then a winner's cheer and a loser's groan; a ping pong sound plays. `id: ping_pong_table` `id: pingpong`
- **Espresso machine**: a drip pot on a cart, then a prosumer machine and grinder, then a chrome bar. `id: espresso`
- **Office printer**: from the Office Floor on, while there's a coffee corner or espresso machine, a printer stands against the wall nearest it. It shows READY, blinks its green light now and then, and every so often hums through a job. It has no stats. When PC LOAD LETTER fires, this same printer jams where it stands (see Nods). After "Take it out back" it's gone while the wreck lies by the door, then a new one takes its place. After "Call the repair line" it's back at once. After "Print less" it stays dead under an OUT OF ORDER sign. `?mock=floor`
- **Plant wall**: two pots, a ladder shelf, then a living wall with a grow light; people water it. `id: plant_wall`
- **Nap pod**: a beanbag (lounge in it, awake and humming), a couch with a blanket, then a sleep pod with a glass canopy (lie down with a zzz). `id: nap_pod`
- **Arcade**: a blue cabinet played standing, a pink cabinet with a stool, then two cabinets and a neon sign; players cheer with sparkles, over arcade sounds. `id: arcade`
- **Standing desks**: a box on a desk, a motorised desk, then a treadmill desk. `id: standing_desk`
- **Whiteboard wall**: a mobile board, a wide board with sticky notes, then a wall-sized kanban. `id: whiteboard_wall`
- **Library nook**: a bookshelf, then an armchair for reading, then a reading nook with lamp and rug. `id: library`
- **Monitoring wall** (Agents era): one screen, then a console, then a tiled video wall with a bar per automation function that flashes red on alarm; overseers stand in front of it. `id: monitoring_wall`
- **Network Operations Center** (once Ops and Security opens; one per office): trouble gets caught sooner and outages end faster. It grows with the office: a pager and a TV on a cart in the garage, a darkened corner with a screen wall and a curved desk on the Office Floor, a full operations floor at HQ. With humans on the glass it needs Security staff (full effect at three); in the Agents era a one-time decision can hand it to agents, who catch more with nobody watching but now and then read a real alert as routine, and that incident lands a step worse. The mode can be switched back and forth, at most once every 26 weeks (a Network Operations Center card in the Ops panel, with a refusal shown when it is too soon). The Security crew sits at it (one on a folding chair by the cart, then chairs at the desk, extras standing behind) while humans watch. With agents watching, the screens scroll agent logs and the chairs stay empty. An outage or an incident that week turns its light strips, screens and floor glow red, lights the beacon at HQ, and gets the crew on their feet. After four quiet weeks, the first of the crew dozes at the desk with a zzz. A "days since last incident" sign counts up and snaps to 0. A misread flashes a green all-clear on every screen before the red lands. On Low there are fewer lit screens and no floor glow. `id: noc`
- **Office robot** (Agents era; one per office; $15,000, $45,000, $120,000): a coffee robot with a charging dock that gives everyone a little stamina and morale recovery. At level 2 it also waters the plants, so Potted Plants help more; level 3 breaks down less. About once a year it breaks and pays nothing that week: it spins in place, gets stuck apologising to a chair, or brings coffee to the empty desk of someone who's away. The next week someone slaps it back to life. Anyone who does that twice earns **Percussive Maintenance** and from then on fixes it on the spot. The staff blame it for automation. Once a third of the work is automated, #random grumbles about "clankers", and anyone whose own job is mostly automated refuses its coffee (they lose its morale lift). Past 60%, someone now and then sabotages it (a traffic cone in its path, decaf in the hopper, unplugged "by accident"); Yak never names who. The first sabotage asks "Who kicked the robot?": a blameless meeting (no sabotage for half a year), googly eyes (sabotage halves for good), or let it go. The Waffle Party and music night also buy it half a year of peace. At low automation #random just likes it ("The robot remembered I take oat milk."). In the office it is a knee-high cream robot with a screen face and a tray: it parks on its dock's pad to charge (the antenna light breathes), then rolls out to bring a coffee to a few people at their desks (a heart over each; a storm cloud when it is serving decaf), and from level 2 now and then waters a plant. Each breakdown shows its cause: amber eyes and spinning by the coffee, nosing into an empty desk's chair, holding a coffee beside the away person's empty desk, stopped at a traffic cone, or slumped dark on its dock with the plug on the floor. The fixer walks (or jogs, from across the office) over, squares up and slaps it on the side of the head; it jolts, its eyes come back cyan with a sparkle, and it heads home. When it's working it joins the incentive parties: at a Waffle Party it rolls up beside the cart with its tray and faces the winner (a heart), and at music night it plays DJ behind the speaker cart, facing the dance floor, nodding on the beat with its antenna light flashing in the genre's colour. It goes round anyone standing in its way, and skips a party it can't reach. While people grumble a sticky note sits on its chest, and googly eyes stay on for good once chosen. `id: office_robot` `id: robot_kicked` `id: percussive` `id: robot` (the slap's staged moment)
- **Server racks**: one rack, two with a cable tray, then a glass-door row with a cold-aisle glow; blinking LEDs. `id: server_rack`
- **Trophy case**: a low shelf, a glass case, then a lit display wall for the Saasies. `id: trophy_case`
- **Desk screens**: code, UI, charts and games by desk; grey for coasting or burnt-out sitters, red during an outage, off when empty.
