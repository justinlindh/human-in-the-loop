# Interface

- **HipCheck**: found a Web 2.0 company with `?eras`. Its blue-grey room chrome, checked speech-bubble emblem, Trebuchet type, ruled message list and presence strip frame browser-testing and wiki chatter. Room labels change while saved channel keys, unread counts, threads and reply actions stay stable. The post picker carries the same skin on desktop and phone. Classic restores Yak. The founding summary shows the historical route and score factor, and Build shows compatibility work in the visible estimate and project card. `id: web2` `id: hipcheck` `id: legacy_compat` `id: web2_best_viewed`

- **AwayIM**: found a dot-com company with `?eras`. Square bevels, a blue title bar, yellow status strip, hourglass emblem, Tahoma controls and serif messages recall desktop instant messaging. Buddy names lead each line with a colon, reactions display as text emoticons, and the Office Chat, Help Desk and Water Cooler labels retain their original saved channel keys. Keyboard replies, post actions and phone controls use the same contracts as Yak. `id: dotcom` `id: awayim`

- **DeskNet**: select Pre-internet with `?eras`. A green terminal title bar, monospace log with bracketed senders, dotted rows, square tabs and `/me` status frame a LAN noticeboard. Reply prompts, keyboard navigation, text reactions, Post picker and phone maximization retain the ordinary chat actions and stored ids. Saved pre-internet careers retain DeskNet without the preview switch. `id: desknet`
- **Physical inventory**: Reports > Inventory and the Build panel's Reports shortcut expose paid batch orders, delivery countdowns and mailed patches by tap or keyboard. Every disabled action gives a visible reason. Product cards separate installed copies from service MRR and link to inventory. Inventory stays accessible after loading a historical save without `?eras` and after entering later chapters. `id: boxed`

- **Advisors**: Marge Tally (CFO), Otto Nwosu (people lead) and Rory Halloran (tech lead) notice what the panels already show and price the trade-off, never give orders: runway ("At this burn we have 11 weeks. I've started pricing smaller chairs."), burnout, comprehension debt, one person holding most of the know-how, juniors without a mentor, a migration coming due, one product paying for everything, a policy unlocked and never tried, a new era just arrived, and, when nothing applies, "Things are fine. I hate it when things are fine." Each notice offers two or three real things you could do about it, each opening the menu where it's done ("Start The Big Refactor" in Build) and landing on the control: "Give Priya lighter work" opens her card with a highlighted suggestion (with a one-line reason) that changes nothing until you tap Confirm, "Send Priya on time off" scrolls to and highlights her Time off button (offered on her card when she is running on empty or burnt out, or when an advisor points at it), "Hire another engineer" opens the Hire tab; they're suggestions, never taken for you. The lightbulb in the top-right chip (or H) shows a dot while their latest notice is unseen and opens a panel with that notice, how long ago it came in when it's been a while, Show me (which only opens the relevant panel) and Not now (which silences that topic until it gets worse), plus a short Earlier list of older notices. When the sim flags a line worth saying out loud, the advisor's face and the line peek in at the screen edge for a few seconds (a tap opens the panel) and the lightbulb glows until you look; at most once every 12 game weeks, never in a week with a decision, never at the fastest speed and never during a spotlight. Settings: On, Quiet (the dot, no peek) or Off (no lightbulb).
- **Title screen**: New Game, Continue, Settings over the live diorama; several save slots, each with Export (saves it as a file) and a two-tap delete; Import a save reads a file into a new slot, says plainly why a file can't load, and when every slot is taken asks which company to replace (a second tap confirms). Settings exports the game in progress. A note says the game saves itself in this browser, so closing the tab is safe and another browser or cleared site data starts fresh (Export keeps a copy): a full card on a first visit, one quiet line once a save exists, and the same words under Saving in Settings. `capture 1-1-title-saves`
- **Welcome back**: Continue opens a recap of where the company stands and what happened last. `capture 1-1-continue-recap`
- **HUD**: cash with the weekly change, the date with the era emblem, brand, know-how, tech debt and (late) fame meters (tech debt shows last week's net change, and its tip and the Reports health card name the biggest sources, or what holds it at zero), speed buttons (1x, 2x, 4x) and a quick mute. Two buttons with left and right turn arrows turn the view a quarter at a time (the same as Q and E): bottom right on desktop, top right under the HUD on phones, hidden while a panel covers the scene; the first build mode shows a bubble pointing at them, gone when you turn or leave build mode. `capture 4-1-hud`
- **Needs strip**: things waiting on the player, most urgent first, each with a click-to-fix and sometimes a one-tap fix.
- **Panels**: Build, Staff, Marketing, Model Vendors, Automation, Policies, Ops and Security, Office and Reports, unlocking left to right as the game goes. `capture 4-3-build-panel` `capture 4-4-reports`
- **NOC mode card**: with a Network Operations Center placed, the Ops panel shows a card with two big buttons, Humans on the glass and Let the agents watch, the status (who is watching and how many weeks until the mode can change again) and what the mode costs or gains. Each button says what its mode does. Every tap goes to the sim, so a switch that is too early or before the choice shows its reason, and that reason takes the toast dock even while a severe toast is up. `id: noc`
- **Decisions and toasts**: decision cards dock right with a light dim; toasts stack top right and dock inside an open panel so they never cover its controls. A toast too long for its box shows a "more" cue and opens in full on a tap. While a spotlight moment plays, other toasts wait for it to end, but one answering the player's own tap or key press shows at once.
- **Moment captions**: a single fading line near the bottom while the printer, the first user test or the consultants play.
- **Scene tips**: hover (or long-press) a person or item in the office for a tooltip; clicking a person opens them in Staff, clicking an item opens its card to move, upgrade or sell.
- **Coach marks**: dismissible tips for the HUD and speed controls.
- **Game over**: a headline, a score breakdown and epilogue lines revealed one at a time. `?mock=ending`
- **Era-start record**: with `?eras`, Reports keeps the chosen later start and era score factor visible, and the ending lists the era factor. These labels are hidden without the switch. The ending also lists the funding factor and incubator cut alongside dilution and the loss factor. HUD, goals, incident history, recap and chart dates follow the saved company's calendar start with or without the switch. `id: chatgbt` `id: agents`
- **Settings**: Auto, Low or High quality, tilt-shift, pause while menus are open, pause on focus loss, "Camera follows big moments", default speed, how much Yak asks for attention, and volume per bus (music, ambience, effects, interface, voices). `capture 4-7-settings`
- **Yak level**: All, Important or Off, in Settings and on a button in Yak's header. Important counts only incidents, wins, bot posts and replies to a post you are waiting on as new; Off keeps Yak shut with no unread count. A reply prompt still shows its Reply mark at every level. Routine posts Yak skipped to keep its pace stay skipped after a reload.
- **Camera**: drag to pan, wheel to zoom, Q and E to turn the view in 90 degree steps.
- **Growth**: a promotion, an earned trait or a trained skill gets a toast with the person's portrait that opens their card; level-ups mark Staff as new, rows show a New pip, and the card lists what grew since you last looked and a growth timeline (levels, promotions, traits, training, a chosen path, becoming a legend) that is saved with the game.
- **Squads**: from the Office Floor or 8 people, Staff gets a Squads tab (a toast says so the week it opens). Each squad is a card: its faces (a star on the lead; dashed "on loan" for anyone doing other work, a teal "upkeep" tag for the engineers left looking after a product they launched), a posting pill (on a project, Maintenance, Support, idle, or "benched · 2w left"), how many are posted, a cohesion bar with the output it adds, and an After launch switch (keep an upkeep crew, or everyone to maintenance). Post squad sends everyone who can go and a toast names who stayed put and why ("Uzma, Tyler: Only engineers do maintenance"). A benched squad shows "Core is free: start something" with Start a product, Post to project and Maintenance. Tap a face to make them lead, move them to another squad, lend them out, take them out, or open their card; tap the name to rename; "..." renames or disbands. New squad picks a name (the dice suggests one) and up to 8 people; joining moves them out of their old squad. Project cards in Build carry a chip ("Core · 4 of 5") that opens the squad, and team rows show each member's squad. The people lead's idle-squad notice opens the tab scrolled to that squad. `id: squadIdle`
- **Incident card**: while an outage runs, a card under the HUD (top right, over the toasts; full width under the HUD on phones) shows the SEV, what broke and why ("Ransomware got past a security posture of 9."), who is on it (tap a face for their card), "Back in ~2 wk" or "Nobody here can fix it" with a Call for help button to Ops, and the running cost in cash, brand and customers. Hide tucks it away for this incident; the tray's outage card brings it back. Responders show "Responding" in Staff and their Doing line says so. At the all-clear a severe incident's postmortem opens under a green "Product is back up · SEV2" header with how long it took, what it cost, and what helped and hurt; a minor one gets a toast that opens the same summary. `id: incident_postmortem`
