# Touch and low-end support

- **Phone layout**: narrow or short touch screens get a stacked layout, "Tap" copy, a tray folded into a badge strip and Yak collapsed.
- **Touch input**: one finger pans, a pinch zooms and pans, a pinch is never a tap, and long-press stands in for hover everywhere.
- **Touch building**: the first tap aims the ghost and shows the reason and adjacency, a second tap on the same spot places.
- **Touch-sized controls**: picker rows grow for fingers, toasts dock two at a time, and quick posts open as a sheet.
- **Low quality**: drawn straight to the screen with no post effects (no ambient occlusion, bloom, tilt-shift or anti-aliasing pass); on a software GPU at half resolution and without the sun's shadows; fewer interior lamps, lighter procedural poses, scenery without trees, clouds, cars or lamps, no typing bed, fewer cheer voices, and staged moments shrink to an emote with nobody walking.
- **Auto quality**: Auto picks Low on a slow or software GPU.
- **Install and offline play**: the public build is an installable app (Chrome's Install app, Add to Home Screen on iPhone and Android) that opens full screen. Installed, it downloads the whole game in the background after the first launch (about 48 MB, with a progress pill and "Ready to play offline" when done, resuming if cut off) and then plays fully offline, every era, model, track and sound included; a normal browser tab downloads nothing unless Settings, Offline, "Download" is tapped. On a launch with a network the installed app looks for a new release, downloads it in the background, then asks "A new version is ready. Restart to update?" (Restart now or Later); with no network it starts as usual from what it has, with no message. The old version keeps working until the new download is complete, and saves carry over. Media: none (an install prompt and the browser's cache, not a scene).
